COMMERCIAL COVENANT

Terms of Service

Last updated: October 2026 • Governing commercial design and digital commissions.

1. Scope of Agreement

These Terms of Service govern all creative commissions, branding projects, editorial print designs, web software development, and educational courses provided by Inklet Studios in Chuka, Kenya.

2. Commission Process & Acceptance

Submission of an online project brief via booking.html initiates a scope evaluation. A commission becomes binding only after Inklet Studios delivers a formal Scope of Work (SOW) and the agreed initial deposit has been settled.

3. Intellectual Property Rights Transfer

We believe in full creative ownership for clients. Upon final payment settlement of all agreed fees:

  • Full exclusive commercial copyright of the final chosen brand mark, print artwork, or custom web source code transfers completely to the client.
  • Preliminary concept sketches, rejected vector variations, and internal studio working files remain the intellectual property of Inklet Studios.
  • Inklet Studios reserves the right to showcase the finalized project within its portfolio, case studies, and design competition entries.

4. Revisions & Scope Adjustments

Every commission includes structured refinement rounds (typically 2 to 3 iterations) as specified in the agreed SOW. Revisions beyond the agreed scope or fundamental changes to the initial brief will be quoted at our standard studio hourly rate.

5. Payment Terms & Milestones

Unless explicitly agreed otherwise in writing, commercial projects operate under milestone stages:

  • Initial Retainer: 50% deposit required prior to project kick-off and research.
  • Final Settlement: 50% balance due upon presentation and approval of final deliverables prior to release of master vector/source archives.

6. Graphic Design Classes & Cancellations

Seats in our Chuka studio cohorts are strictly capped at 12 students to maintain direct mentorship. Tuition settlements are refundable up to 7 calendar days before cohort launch, minus a 10% administrative fee.

7. Applicable Law & Jurisdiction

These terms are governed by the Laws of the Republic of Kenya. Any disputes arising shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of Kenya, with parties agreeing to pursue amicable mediation first.
