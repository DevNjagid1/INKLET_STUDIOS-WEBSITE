Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Compliant with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

1. Commitment to Privacy

Inklet Studios (“we”, “us”, “our”), headquartered in Chuka, Tharaka Nithi County, Kenya, respects your personal dignity and privacy. This policy outlines our collection, storage, and processing practices regarding information submitted through this website.

2. Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to execute design commissions and academy courses:

  • Project Brief Submissions: Client name, email, phone number, organization, service discipline, scope details, target budget, and desired deadline.
  • Direct Contact Messages: Name, email, phone number, subject, and message contents.
  • Design Academy Enrollments: Student name, email, phone number, current experience level, and learning goals.
  • Local Browser Storage: We store your chosen theme preference (inklet_theme: dark or light) in your browser’s local storage to prevent visual flashes upon page navigation.

3. Lawful Basis and Purpose of Processing

Your data is processed strictly for legitimate business objectives:

  • Reviewing, pricing, and scheduling custom creative commissions.
  • Issuing cryptographically unique booking references and transactional email receipts.
  • Registering students into limited-seat cohorts at our studio in Chuka.
  • Maintaining security audit trails to protect against automated spam and unauthorized tampering.

4. Data Retention & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or marketing networks. Information is retained for the duration of the active client relationship or statutory tax and accounting retention periods in Kenya.

5. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request rectification of inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Request deletion of your information, subject to statutory retention obligations.

To exercise your rights or ask questions regarding your privacy, contact our studio administrator at:
