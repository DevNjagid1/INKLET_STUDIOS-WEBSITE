Brand Identity & Logos • 2026 • Kipawa Coffee Co. (Sample Project)

Kipawa Coffee Roasters — Visual Identity

A grounded, heritage-rich identity for a specialty highlands coffee brand rooted in Mount Kenya foothills.

DISCIPLINE Brand Identity & Packaging
TOOLS & MEDIUM Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, Glyphs
LOCATION & YEAR Chuka / Mt. Kenya • 2026
ACTION Commission Similar

01. The Commission Brief

Develop a complete brand identity that honors smallholder farming heritage while appealing to international specialty coffee buyers and contemporary Nairobi cafes.

02. Creative Direction & Concept

Restrained earth tones, warm ivory paper stock, bespoke linework illustration of Mount Kenya contours, and timeless serif typography.

03. The Design Process

Extensive research into Tharaka Nithi and Meru agricultural cooperatives, 40+ pencil thumbnails of coffee cherry geometry, custom type vectorization, packaging die-line testing.

04. Deliverables & Verified Impact

Deliverables: Brand guidelines, primary mark, packaging labels, kraft paper bag stamping guides, and barista apparel specs.

Verified Outcomes: Sample project showcase: Demonstrates artisanal coffee packaging and heritage storytelling system.
